Introduction

Project Overview

The primary goal of this academic prototype is to observe operational traffic patterns in urban environments and demonstrate a very basic system for improvement. Traditional fixed-time intersection controllers fail to adapt to asymmetric diurnal traffic surges, causing unnecessary vehicle idling, fuel wastage, and transit delays. This prototype leverages open-source computer vision (YOLOv8 and ByteTrack) combined with classical transportation engineering delay models (Webster's uniform delay) to provide real-time queue classification and adaptive green interval recommendations.

Architecture Flow

System Pipeline & Methodology

  1. Input: Video source ingestion from file, sample, or webcam.
  2. Detection: YOLOv8n object detection running on CPU, filtering COCO vehicle classes (car, motorcycle, bus, truck).
  3. Tracking & Counting: ByteTrack persistent association with 2D cross-product line crossing logic.
  4. Density: Spatial point-in-polygon containment analysis across 4 approach zones.
  5. Decision: Congestion threshold checks and Webster uniform delay signal optimization.
  6. Output: Server-Sent Events (SSE) telemetry feed to the live interactive web dashboard.

Constraints

System Limitations

  • • Adverse Weather & Lighting: Nighttime darkness, heavy rain, and lens glare reduce object detection confidence and tracking accuracy.
  • • Auto-Rickshaw Classification: Auto-rickshaws and e-rickshaws are not native COCO classes and are mapped to generic cars or motorcycles.
  • • Mixed Traffic Density: Unlane-disciplined heterogeneous traffic with severe occlusion makes individual bounding box separation challenging.
  • • Indicative Delay Model: Waiting time is an illustrative Webster uniform estimate, not a certified physical sensor measurement.
  • • No Physical Actuation: As an academic prototype, this system never directly controls physical road signal controllers.
Roadmap

Future Scope

  • • Physical Controller Integration: Connecting output green suggestions to standard NTCIP / SCATS traffic light controller protocols.
  • • Edge Hardware Optimization: Compiling models to TensorRT or ONNX Runtime for low-power Raspberry Pi 5 or Jetson deployment.
  • • Emergency Priority: Audio siren classification and optical strobe detection for automated emergency green waves.
  • • Smart City C4I Integration: REST and MQTT APIs for unified integration into municipal Intelligent Transport Systems (ITS).
Ethics & Governance

Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Privacy by Design: This system processes video frames strictly in volatile RAM memory. No license plate recognition (ALPR), facial recognition, or biometric identification is performed or stored. Raw video frames are never written to the SQLite database or saved permanently to disk. Uploaded test files are automatically deleted after 24 hours.

Educational Terms: This software is provided as an open-source educational prototype for the IDEA Lab-II engineering curriculum. It is provided “as is” without warranty of any kind and is not designed or certified for real-world municipal traffic actuation.
